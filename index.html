Research

What the lab works on

The Thomas Lab works at the forefront of technology to deliver cutting-edge research, using machinery to automate chemistry. Based in the School of Chemistry at the University of Glasgow and embedded in the Cronin Group, the lab develops Digital Chemistry and automation — from automated synthesis platforms for hazardous molecules through to reaction monitoring, computer vision, and drug discovery.

Its research draws together three strands: digital chemistry and chemputation, organic synthesis, and the supramolecular chemistry of artificial molecular machines — fuelled systems whose nanoscopic motion can be harnessed for logic, cargo delivery, and macroscopic function.
